# CareHome GRC & ISMS Portfolio – Asset Register

## 1. Project Overview

This asset register is part of my Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) portfolio project, designed around a fictional UK care-home organisation.

The purpose of this register is to identify information assets, document their business purpose and ownership, assess their confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements, and record key security controls.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Identify and document key information assets.
- Map assets to business processes and organisational functions.
- Assign an owner to each asset.
- Classify information according to its sensitivity.
- Assess confidentiality, integrity and availability (CIA).
- Determine asset criticality.
- Document relevant security controls.
- Provide a foundation for risk assessment and ISO 27001-aligned ISMS development.

## 3. Workbook Structure

The Excel workbook contains four worksheets:

| Worksheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Documents the main organisational functions and their responsibilities. |
| Process Map | Maps business processes to functions, information, owners and supporting systems. |
| Asset Register | Records 36 fictional information assets and their security-related attributes. |
| Read Me | Explains the workbook's purpose, assumptions and limitations. |

## 4. Asset Register Fields

The Asset Register worksheet contains the following fields:

- **Asset ID:** Unique identifier for each asset.
- **Asset Name:** Name of the information asset or system.
- **Asset Type:** Category of the asset.
- **Business Process:** Process supported by the asset.
- **Information/Function:** Business purpose or function.
- **Classification:** Information classification, such as Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
- **Asset Owner:** Role responsible for the asset.
- **Primary Data/Content:** Main information stored or processed.
- **Confidentiality:** Assesses the need to prevent unauthorised disclosure.
- **Integrity:** Assesses the need to protect information from unauthorised changes.
- **Availability:** Assesses the need to keep the asset accessible when required.
- **Criticality:** Indicates the asset's business importance.
- **Key Controls:** Illustrative security measures relevant to the asset.

## 6. Methodology

The register uses a structured, qualitative approach to asset identification and classification.

1. Identify assets that support business processes.
2. Map each asset to its relevant process and organisational function.
3. Assign an asset owner or responsible role.
4. Record the information held or processed.
5. Apply an initial information classification.
6. Assess confidentiality, integrity and availability using Low, Medium or High ratings.
7. Assign a separate criticality rating.
8. Document relevant security controls for further review.

The CIA ratings and criticality levels are initial portfolio assumptions. They should be validated with asset owners and business stakeholders before being used for operational decisions.

## 7. GRC and ISO 27001 Relevance

This register demonstrates practical application of asset management principles that support an Information Security Management System (ISMS).

It can support:

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022-aligned information security risk assessment.
- Identification of information security risks and existing controls.
- Access management and periodic access reviews.
- Business continuity and backup planning.
- Supplier and third-party risk management.
- Security policy development.
- Internal audit preparation and evidence tracking.

The register is intended to support risk identification and treatment planning. It does not, by itself, demonstrate ISO 27001 compliance or certification.

## 8. Key Limitations

- All assets and organisational details are fictional.
- CIA ratings and criticality levels are preliminary assumptions.
- Listed security controls have not been independently tested or verified.
- Asset ownership and control effectiveness would require validation in a real organisation.
- No real resident, patient or employee information is included.
